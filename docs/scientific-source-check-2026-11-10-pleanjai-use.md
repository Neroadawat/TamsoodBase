# Thai FAME use-phase source screen — 10 November 2026

## Finding

The Springer book record confirms the 2013 chapter *Sustainability Assessment of Palm Biodiesel Production in Thailand* by Thapat Silalertruksa and Shabbir H. Gheewala, pages 29–49, DOI [10.1007/978-3-642-34519-7_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-34519-7_2). Its Section 2.3.5 reports pickup/light-duty diesel vehicle use-phase emissions per litre of biodiesel: 2.78 kg CO2, 7.5 g CO, 29 g NOx, and 1.3 g particulate matter. It treats most combustion CO2 as biogenic and reports 0.15 kg CO2/L for the non-biogenic methanol share. These values are reported secondarily by the chapter, which attributes them to Pleanjai (2008).

The chapter bibliography identifies the cited work as Somporn Pleanjai's 2008 PhD dissertation, *Integrated environmental assessment of biodiesel from palm oil*, at the Joint Graduate School of Energy and Environment, King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi. Exact-title and institution-domain searches in this review did not locate an official full-text repository item. The dissertation and underlying engine test therefore remain `DISCOVERED_NOT_DIRECTLY_VERIFIED`; the chapter text was read from a third-party PDF mirror and was not saved, hashed, or visually reviewed.

## Decision for TamsoodBase

Keep the reported factors as contextual evidence only. They do not close the current 2020 FAME case's use-phase gate: the 2020 functional unit is 1,000 MJ, while these reported values are per litre; no verified LHV/density bridge or matching vehicle/test conditions connect them. The source is also not cleared for redistribution or external-model transmission. Status: `HOLD_CONTEXT`, `NOT_CLEARED`.

No inventory row or lifecycle total was changed; `CO2e_total` remains unchanged. No data was added to production or Gemini corpora. Evidence: [`scientific-source-check-2026-11-10-pleanjai-use.json`](../evidence/scientific-source-check-2026-11-10-pleanjai-use.json).
