# Thai Agro Energy 2021: production and wastewater evidence

On 10 November 2026, I reviewed the indexed text of Thai Agro Energy PCL's Annual Report 2021 (Form 56-1 One Report) for a Thai ethanol case with production and wastewater data reported for the same year. This is a separate company and site from TSM/Thai Sugar Ethanol; it is an operational reference case only.

## Source-reported values and locators

- The report identifies the plant at Nong Makhamong, Dan Chang District, Suphanburi Province and describes two ethanol production lines. Line 1 uses molasses; Line 2 can use molasses, cassava, raw sugar, and other planned feedstocks (printed report pp. 13–14; extracted PDF lines 541–548, 619–632).
- The production table reports actual 2021 output of 23,118,435 L for Line 1 and 62,164,766 L for Line 2, total 85,283,201 L (printed p. 14; PDF lines 550–599). The report describes the final alcohol purity as 99.8% by volume (printed p. 15; PDF lines 612–617).
- The environmental section reports 734,615 m³ of spent-wash wastewater generated in 2021 (printed p. 41; PDF lines 1774–1780). It also says wastewater supply to the digester could support biogas production at about 83% of total capacity in January–June and 47% in July–December, with Line 1 stopped during July–December. These are reported capacity-utilization descriptions, not measured biogas or methane volumes.
- The report says all plant process wastewater is sent to anaerobic digestion, after which water is used as fertilizer or evaporates naturally, with no drainage outside (printed p. 39; PDF lines 1759–1769). It reports electricity use of about 28,000,000 and generation of 19,000,000 with units printed as “kW”; because energy units are dimensionally questionable in this context, these values are retained as reported and excluded from energy-intensity calculations.
- The company says it was beginning its GHG assessment using data for 2021–2022 for a planned TGO application; this report does not give a facility-specific mass of CH₄ emitted or released from the digester (printed p. 42; PDF lines 1792–1799).

The official 2022 annual report supplies another internally matched plant year: actual production was 14,022,334 L on Line 1 and 59,494,253 L on Line 2, totaling 73,516,587 L; spent-wash generation was 614,816 m³. This yields a team-derived ratio of `614,816 × 1,000 ÷ 73,516,587 = 8.36295624 L spent wash/L reported ethanol`. The report says wastewater supply could support biogas production at about 92% of capacity in January–March, treatment stopped in April when both lines temporarily ceased production, and May–December supply was about 54% as Line 1 stopped. These are capacity/utilization descriptions, not measured biogas quantities. The same report states steam production of 256,777.43 tonnes, activity fuel use of 82,590 L diesel, 6,386 L E10, and 92,000 L fuel oil, and electricity values of 25,000,000 kW total (15,000,000 kW generated; 10,000,000 kW purchased). Because the electricity unit is printed as kW, it is not converted to energy or intensity. Locators: production table PDF lines 671–717; wastewater, operating periods, steam/fuels/electricity PDF lines 1747–1767.


## Follow-up against the later TGO-certified company reporting

The company's 2025 Form 56-1 report summarizes organization-level GHG assessments and gives totals (t CO₂e) for reporting years 2022, 2023, and 2024 as 148,452; 153,080; and 257,665. Scope 1/2/3 components are 143,210/4,350/892; 146,338/5,603/1,139; and 250,339/6,062/1,264, respectively. Each year's three scope values add exactly to its reported total (printed report p. 37; indexed PDF lines 1503–1549).

The 2022 annual report provides the 2021 base-year corporate assessment: Scope 1 = 143,210, Scope 2 = 4,350, and Scope 3 = 892 t CO₂e; the company reports TGO approval on 23 August 2022. The three scopes sum to 148,452 t CO₂e. The 2025 report's later-year table covers reporting years 2022–2024 and therefore does not repeat the base-year total; it is incorrect to describe 2021 as undisclosed across the company's reports. These CFO totals are organization-level context and do not match the separate 2022 plant operating-year data by year. Neither report provides gas-by-gas quantities, a CH₄ mass, or a site-only attribution for those scope totals (2022 printed report p. 42; indexed PDF lines 1770–1798; 2025 printed report p. 37; indexed PDF lines 1503–1549).

The current TGO public registry has a record named “ผลิตเอทานอลเพื่อใช้เป็นเชื้อเพลิง” (“ethanol production for fuel”), but explicitly classifies it as Carbon Footprint for Organization (CFO), certificate `TGO CFO FY25-02-033`, approved 22 April 2026 and expiring 21 April 2027. This is organization-level certification metadata, not a product CFP value per litre and not the detailed 2021 base-year inventory. The activity name must not be mistaken for proof of a product functional unit or life-cycle result ([TGO registry record](https://thaicarbonlabel.tgo.or.th/index.php?action=WkdWMFlXbHM&lang=TH&mod=YjNKbllXNXBlbUYwYVc5dVgyRndjSEp2ZG1Gcw&param=TXpBeE5EQT0)).

These are organization-wide GHG results in t CO₂e, not product-system LCI results. Their reporting boundary is not sufficiently detailed here to establish a facility-only result, and the source does not identify what share comes from wastewater methane. Although the 2021 base-year CFO, plant output, and spent-wash volume refer to the same year and company, do not divide the CFO total by ethanol output or use it to infer CH₄ or kg CO₂e/L without evidence that the CFO boundary and product attribution match.

## Team calculation

Using only the same-report, same-year plant totals:

`734,615 m³ × 1,000 L/m³ ÷ 85,283,201 L ethanol = 8.613830055 L spent wash/L reported ethanol`

The reported total output also reconciles arithmetically: `23,118,435 + 62,164,766 = 85,283,201 L`. This is a team-derived, plant-average wastewater-to-product volume ratio, not a value reported directly by the company. It is not feedstock-specific; Line 2 can use different feedstocks, and the report itself says wastewater/biogas operation depends on raw materials and production line status. It uses nominal product volume at the plant's stated 99.8% v/v purity, not a corrected pure-ethanol functional unit.

For 2022, the separately reported line outputs reconcile to `14,022,334 + 59,494,253 = 73,516,587 L`; the corresponding plant-average ratio is `614,816 m³ × 1,000 L/m³ ÷ 73,516,587 L = 8.36295624 L spent wash/L reported ethanol`. This remains a derived volume ratio, not methane intensity or an LCI emission factor. Do not average it with the 2021 ratio without a defined temporal aggregation rule.

The report separately lists company 2021 conversion-ethanol sales as 85.352 million L (printed p. 13; PDF lines 513–525), 68,799 L above the production-table total. Production and sales are distinct metrics; the difference is not reconciled in the report and must not be silently adjusted or interpreted as a measurement error.

## Relevance and limits

This supplies one useful Thai operational example for the wastewater-treatment stage, including a same-year production denominator. It does **not** close the project's TSM wastewater/methane gap and cannot be transferred as a factor to TSM, another plant, or molasses-only ethanol. No CH₄ mass, biogas volume/composition, methane leakage/slip, or matched production-period mass balance is reported here; therefore no methane intensity or CO₂e is calculated. The 83%/47% statements are not quantities of gas and are not used as such.
