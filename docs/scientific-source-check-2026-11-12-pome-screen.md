# POME methane source screen — 12 November 2026

## Scope

One bounded screen for evidence that might close the Thai FAME-chain wastewater/methane gap. The required fit is Thai foreground data with a traceable facility, an appropriate reporting period, methane or a defensible wastewater inventory, and production quantity matched to that same facility and period. This screen does **not** alter any inventory value or lifecycle result.

## Candidate screened and rejected for this case

Putro (2022), “Emissions of CH4 and CO2 from wastewater of palm oil mills: A real contribution to increase the greenhouse gas and its potential as renewable energy sources,” *Environment and Natural Resources Journal* 20(1), 61–72, DOI [10.32526/ennrj/20/202100149](https://doi.org/10.32526/ennrj/20/202100149). The journal-hosted article is available at <https://ph02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/ennrj/article/download/245510/166413/863558>.

The paper describes direct chamber measurements at a palm-oil mill in South Sumatra, **Indonesia**, not Thailand (printed pp. 61, 63–64). It reports methane/CO2 rates and derives POME GHG coefficients, including 0.556 t CO2-eq/t CPO (printed pp. 61, 68–69). The same paper says the coefficient is an initial estimate and requests broader studies across mills and operating conditions (printed p. 69). The measured methane and the CPO-normalized coefficient are therefore not transferable observations for the Thai FAME foreground chain. No matched Thai facility or reporting period was found in this source.

Decision: `REJECTED_SCOPE_MISMATCH_FOR_THAI_CASE`; do not import the emission factor, do not use it to populate missing methane, and do not use it to calculate the team’s FAME functional unit. This source can only be cited as a methodological/external comparison if that is useful. Redistribution rights were not established in this screen, so no full-text or table content is packaged for the website or RAG.

## Related result checked, not new usable evidence

Search surfaced Enström et al. (2019), DOI [10.1007/s10668-018-0181-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10668-018-0181-4), whose measurements were at a mill in Perak, **Malaysia**, during parts of 2017. Although the paper reports approximately 156 t CPO/day and approximately 430 m3 POME/day for that mill (Methods, §2), its geography is outside the Thai-only case. It does not close the Thai case and must not be substituted. It is also already screened as a nonmatching source in the research context; this note records why the surfaced result is excluded, not a fresh data extraction.

The Thai 2012 study by Kaewmai et al. and Thai 2015 study by Saswattecha et al. remain contextual/aggregate sources already screened in project records. They do not establish a matched methane measurement and actual output for the specific FAME-chain provider and period. Do not treat search snippets or published GHG intensity as raw methane inventory.
